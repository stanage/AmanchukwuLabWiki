==================================
Base Bath Standard Operating Procedure
==================================

The base bath is a powerful tool designed to etch away trace amounts of impurities from glassware. It is not a general dishwashing station; improper use can damage glassware or pose a significant safety risk.

-----------------------------------------------------------
Safety Precautions & Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Because the base bath contains a highly corrosive alkaline solution, you must strictly adhere to the following safety protocols before and during use:

* **Required Attire:** Always wear a lab coat and safety goggles.
* **Double Glove Mandate:** You must wear double gloves: a pair of standard nitrile gloves, topped with a pair of long, chemical-resistant gloves.
* **Handling Warning:** Proceed slowly and with extreme care when adding or removing items. Glassware becomes highly slippery once it contacts the base solution.
* **First Aid:** If the base solution contacts your skin, immediately rinse the area with a large volume of flowing water.

-----------------------------------------
Loading Glassware into the Base Bath
-----------------------------------------

Glassware must be thoroughly pre-cleaned before it ever enters the base bath.

.. rubric:: Step A: Pre-Cleaning (Crucial)

* Ensure there are no visible stains on the item.
* Use targeted solvents for initial cleaning:
  
  * **Hexane:** Use to rinse away vacuum grease.
  * **Water:** Use to dissolve and remove salts.
  * **Surfactants & Brushes:** Use when necessary to scrub away debris.

* After proper pre-cleaning, give the glassware a thorough rinse with ethanol before placing it into the bath.

.. rubric:: Step B: Submersion

1. Immerse the glassware slowly into the bath.
2. Ensure the base solution flows completely into all internal cavities so the entire inner surface is treated.
3. Do not stack glassware on top of each other.

.. rubric:: Step C: Duration

* An overnight soak is typically sufficient to clean the glass.
* For large pieces of glassware that cannot be fully submerged, soak for one night, then rotate the item to treat the remaining half for a second night.

------------------------------------------------
Retrieving and Rinsing Cleaned Glassware
------------------------------------------------

Follow these steps to safely remove and neutralize the glassware:

1. **Transfer Safely:** Use an empty plastic box to manage the transfer. Lift the glassware out of the base solution, allow the excess liquid to drip back into the bath, and place the glassware directly into the empty plastic box.
2. **Transport to Sink:** Carry the plastic box over to the sink.
3. **Thorough Rinsing:** 
   
   * Rinse the glassware (including the inside, outside, and any jackets) thoroughly with tap water three times.
   * Follow this by rinsing with Deionized (DI) water three times.

4. **Inspect the Cleanliness:** After the final DI water rinse, look for a smooth, homogeneous layer of water clinging to the surface of the glass. The absence of water sheeting or droplet formation indicates the glassware is completely clean.
5. **Drying:** Place the cleaned glassware directly into the oven, or transport it using a clean secondary container to prevent re-contamination.
