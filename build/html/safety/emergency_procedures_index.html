Emergency Procedures

While rigorous adherence to laboratory standard operating procedures is designed to minimize risk, we must remain constantly prepared for unforeseen incidents. In any high-consequence research environment handling reactive chemicals, compressed gases, and complex electronic instrumentation, a rapid and coordinated response to emergencies is paramount to ensuring personal safety and mitigating structural hazards. Every member of the laboratory is required to review these protocols regularly and know exactly whom to contact when an emergency arises.

Note

Always prioritize personal safety and full evacuation before addressing infrastructure concerns.

Essential First Aid & Spill Responses

  • Inhalation / Ingestion: Remove the victim to fresh air immediately and contact a physician.

  • Skin / Eye Exposure: Flush with copious amounts of water for 15 minutes (use the emergency eyewash for eyes) and strip off contaminated clothing.

  • Small Spills (<1 L non-toxic): Clean from the perimeter inward using absorbents if comfortable doing so.

  • Large Spills (>1 L or High Hazard): Evacuate the lab immediately, warn others, and dial UCPD (123 / 773-702-8181).

  • Fire Emergency: Attempt to extinguish using the PASS method only if safe to do so; otherwise, activate the emergency wall pull station and evacuate.
