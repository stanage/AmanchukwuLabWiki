Chemical Safety

Research within the lab routinely utilizes a diverse array of chemical reagents, solvents, and specialty mixtures that present severe physical and toxicological hazards. Because exposure to these materials can result in catastrophic injuries, chronic health issues, or laboratory fires, a proactive approach to hazard identification and risk mitigation is mandatory.

This section serves as a repository for our laboratory’s chemical safety protocols. It outlines strict guidelines for the handling of volatile Particularly Hazardous Substances (PHS), step-by-step procedures for preparing and utilizing highly reactive cleaning solutions, and the operational boundaries required for manipulating specialized chemical species safely. Compliance with the engineering controls, administrative protocols, and personal protective equipment (PPE) detailed herein is a prerequisite for all chemical work.

For a comprehensive list of all laboratory chemicals, please consult our Chemical Inventory on Box.
