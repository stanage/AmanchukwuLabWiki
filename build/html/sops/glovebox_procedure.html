Glovebox Standard Operating Procedure

The glovebox maintains a highly controlled, inert atmosphere (Argon gas) designed for working with air- and moisture-sensitive materials. Strict compliance with these guidelines is mandatory to prevent system contamination and experimental failure. The lab is equipped with three gloveboxes — GB1, GB2, and GB3 — each with a dedicated antechamber for transferring materials in and out of the main chamber. The antechamber is a critical component that allows for the evacuation of air and moisture from incoming items before they enter the glovebox environment.

Note

  • Mandatory Training: You must be fully trained by the current glovebox representative before using this equipment. To identify the representative, consult the Lab Responsibilities Excel Sheet.

Critical Safety & Environmental Rules

  • Inert Atmosphere Limits: Under NO circumstances should you introduce water or allow ambient air to enter the glovebox.

  • Purity Thresholds: The oxygen and moisture content must always be maintained at less than 0.1 ppm. If levels rise above this threshold, stop work and contact the glovebox representative immediately.

  • Moisture Ban: Do not transfer any products, chemicals, or equipment containing residual moisture into the system. Everything must be completely dry.

  • Vial/Container Requirements: Ensure all containers, vials, or product packages being transferred inside are partially open to allow proper vacuum extraction of trapped air. Ensure any loose powders are physically secure so they do not spill during evacuation.

Operating Procedure (Antechamber Transfer Workflow)

Follow this cycle exactly every time you bring items into the glovebox main chamber:

  1. Load the Antechamber Place your dry, partially open, and secure products into the glovebox antechamber.

  2. First Cycle

    1. Pull a vacuum on the antechamber.

    2. Hold the vacuum for exactly 5 minutes (utilize the physical timers located next to the glovebox).

    3. Vent the antechamber back to pressure.

  3. Repeat Cycles Repeat steps a through c two more times. You must complete a TOTAL of 3 vacuum-and-vent cycles before opening the inner door.

  4. Chamber Transfer Open the inner door, transfer your samples cleanly into the main glovebox chamber, and securely close the inner door.

  5. Idle State While actively working inside the glovebox, pull a partial vacuum in the antechamber to maintain a pressure buffer.
