Gas Safety
==========

Compressed gases and toxic chemical vapors represent unique, high-consequence risk vectors within our lab. Because gas leaks can rapidly displace oxygen, cause sudden toxicity, or create severe fire hazards without visual warning, strict engineering controls, proper storage, and continuous monitoring are mandatory. This section outlines the essential safety protocols for handling high-risk gases such as carbon monoxide (CO) and hydrogen (|H2|).
