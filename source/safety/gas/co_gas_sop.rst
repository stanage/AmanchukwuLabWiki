=============================
Carbon Monoxide (CO) Gas SOP
=============================

This page outlines the essential safety controls and operational boundaries for working with Carbon Monoxide (CO) gas in our lab.

-------------------------
Quick Reference Parameters
-------------------------

.. list-table:: 
   :widths: 40 60
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Parameter
     - Allowed Threshold / Condition
   * - **Authorized Location**
     - Hicks O-603 (Designated ducted benchtop fume hood only)
   * - **Maximum Allowed Exposure**
     - 50 ppm
   * - **Operating Conditions**
     - Room temperature, 0–10 PSI, low flow (< 50 sccm)
   * - **Target Application**
     - Carrier gas and reagent for CO electrochemical reduction

--------------------------
Mandatory Safety Controls
--------------------------

* **Access Restriction:** You must receive specialized hands-on training from the Lab Safety Contact before entering or using the CO workstation.
* **PPE Requirement:** Nitrile gloves, safety glasses, a flame-resistant lab coat, and your personal wearable CO detector.
* **Cylinder Storage:** Store cylinders upright, firmly chained to a wall/rack, locked up, and away from any heat or ignition sources.

.. rubric:: Mandatory Dual Monitoring

* You must wear an active portable CO detector within your breathing zone at all times.
* You must verify that the stationary CO monitor adjacent to the fume hood is active and functioning before opening any gas lines.

------------------
Emergency Response
------------------

.. danger::
   **IF AN ALARM SOUNDS:** Evacuate the laboratory immediately. From a safe distance, contact the PI or Lab Safety Contact. Call the UCPD at **123** (campus phone) or **773-702-8181** (non-campus phone).

* **Inhalation:** Move the victim to fresh air immediately and consult a physician.
* **Skin/Eye Contact:** Flush with copious amounts of water for 15 minutes (use the emergency eyewash for eyes) and consult a physician.
* **Large Spills/Fire:** Evacuate the lab immediately, pull the emergency fire alarm station, and contact UCPD.
