==========================================
Guidelines for Running Samples in the SMCF
==========================================

The Soft Matter Characterization Facility (SMCF) in ERC operates under strict access phases. Our laboratory coordinates all experimental workflows through a single authorized representative to maintain compliance and fair instrument booking thresholds.

* **Lab Representative:** Priya is the approved person with requisite facility access.
* **Approved Instruments:** TGA, DSC, and FTIR.
* **Operational Shift Window:** Sundays from 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM. This is the only physical window allowed for sample setup.
* **Booking Constraint:** The SMCF strictly mandates that instruments can only be reserved a maximum of 3 days in advance.

Request Workflow and Deadlines
==============================

Because instrument time is highly competitive, you must follow this exact sequence to request sample analysis:

* **Initial Consultation:** Before submitting any requests, you must schedule a meeting with Priya. She will review instrument functionality, limitations, and help design your custom program settings. Future modifications to an existing program layout require a follow-up consultation. Priya will reject samples if experimental goals are unclear.
* **Submission Deadline:** Request your required SMCF time by Thursdays at 12:00 PM. This ensures Priya can prepare for the rolling booking window that opens on Friday. Late requests will miss that week's shift slot entirely.
* **Sample Readiness:** You must have physical samples prepared and ready before submitting a request. Do not request time based on speculative preparation schedules.
* **Queue Logic:** Samples are processed strictly on a first-come, first-served basis. If demand exceeds shift capacity, Priya will manage a standard wait-list.
* **Physical Staging:** When your reservation slot arrives, your samples must be clearly labeled and placed in an obvious, accessible lab location. If your samples cannot be located within 5 minutes, your slot is forfeited to the next person on the wait-list.
* **Data Retrieval:** All resulting data files are hosted on Box. Priya will establish a personalized sub-folder under your name within the shared SMCF directory.

Required Submission Metadata
============================

When requesting a run, you must provide the following five pieces of information to Priya:

* Precise sample identity.
* Environmental sensitivities (e.g., air, moisture, heat thresholds).
* Specific instrument programs, heating profiles, or configuration files to execute.
* Exact target filename for the data folder.
* A brief description of expected features (e.g., specific FTIR peaks or thermal transition locations). This allows Priya to instantly assess sample stability and instrument baseline health.

SMCF Instrument Directory
=========================

1. Fourier-Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR)
-------------------------------------------------

* **Application:** Primarily used to determine the chemical structural profile of compounds.
* **Sample States:** Accommodates both solid and liquid matrices.
* **Material Requirements:** Non-destructive technique; requires only ~10–15 mg of sample. Because it is non-destructive, the exact same sample mass can be recovered and used for subsequent TGA or DSC testing.
* **Atmospheric Controls:** Standard runs are done in air. Inert operations are technically possible using a nitrogen () purge (not Argon), but require an explicit, pre-arranged joint consultation with Priya and the SMCF Director, Phil, to configure.

2. Thermogravimetric Analysis (TGA)
-----------------------------------

* **Application:** Evaluates thermal degradation profiles by heating a specimen at a fixed ramp rate and recording weight loss as a function of temperature.
* **Sample States:** Strictly limited to solid-state samples. Do not submit liquids.
* **Material Requirements:** Destructive technique; requires ~10 mg of sample.
* **Atmospheric Warning:** There is no inert option for this instrument; samples are analyzed under ambient air.
* **Automation Delay:** The TGA uses an auto-sampler queue. Priya will load the deck, initiate the automated program, and leave the station. Consequently, your samples may sit exposed to ambient room air for several hours before their specific testing slot triggers. Account for this if your sample is mildly air-sensitive.

3. Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC)
------------------------------------------

* **Application:** Measures fundamental thermal transitions including glass transitions (), melting points (), and crystallization kinetics ().
* **Sample States:** Accommodates both solid and liquid samples.
* **Material Requirements:** Destructive technique; requires ~5–10 mg of sample.
* **Atmospheric Controls:** Can be configured to run in ambient air or inert gas.

Special Protocol: Inert DSC Sample Preparation
==============================================

DSC samples are mechanically crimped into small aluminum pans using a specialized press tool to form an air-tight seal. If your samples require inert gas protection during analysis, you must execute the preparation steps yourself inside the glovebox:

* **Coordination:** You must notify Priya well in advance if you require an inert run. Because glovebox pan-pressing is time-consuming, the researcher is responsible for cell assembly so that Priya can maximize instrument uptime.
* **Material Transfer:** On your scheduled reservation day, meet Priya outside our lab. She will bring the physical press tool and aluminum pans directly to you from the SMCF.
* **Glovebox Assembly:** Bring the press and pans through the antechamber into your glovebox. If you are a first-time user, Priya will provide preparation training and show you how to record the sample mass accurately before pressing.
* **Air Option Alternative:** If your samples are stable in ambient air, you do not need to perform this protocol; Priya can handle the standard pan-pressing step directly at the SMCF facility.
