=============================================
Coin Cell Assembly SOPs (Molten Salt Systems)
=============================================

This page details the standard operating procedures for assembling three types of molten salt coin cells: **Li Metal Full Cells**, **Li|Al Cells**, and **Li|Li Cells**.

Shared Lithium Preparation Workflow
===================================

Before assembling any specific cell stack, prepare the lithium metal disc components according to the following protocol:

.. Sequence
   These initial prep steps must be followed exactly to prevent air-contamination of the lithium metal and to ensure a perfect 12 mm punch.

1. **Crimper Check:** Confirm the electronic setting and pressure (target: 950) on the coin cell crimper.
2. **Cutting:** Cut a strip of lithium metal from stock using scissors, cutting only what is immediately required to minimize material waste.
3. **Surface Oxide Cleaning:** Thoroughly brush both sides of the cut lithium metal strip with a dedicated cleaning brush to expose shiny metal.
4. **Disc Punching:** Align a 12 mm punch cutter with the side edge of the cleaned lithium strip (to maximize material utilization). Press down firmly and give it a slight twist to cleanly separate the lithium disc.
5. **Extraction:** Carefully grab the outer waste edge of the cut lithium strip to detach the punched 12 mm disc from the cutter.
6. **Scrap Mitigation:** Save any unused pieces of cut lithium for later use. If the remaining scraps are completely spent or contaminated, immediately deposit them into the designated Li waste bottle.

..

Specific Cell Stack Assembling
==============================

Choose the appropriate stack sequence depending on the cell architecture you are building:

1. Li Metal Full Coin Cells with Molten Salt
--------------------------------------------

**Pre-assembly Requirements:**
* Measure and record the dry mass of your cathodes before beginning stack assembly.
* **Thermal Setup:** Place a hotplate inside the glovebox and set the temperature based on the specific melting point of your salt samples. Place the molten salt vial onto the hotplate to melt the electrolyte.

**Assembly Steps:**

1. Lay down the cell top part (-) flat on the assembly surface.
2. Place one spring directly into the center of the top part.
3. Take a spacer, align a punched 12 mm lithium metal disc exactly at its center, and press down firmly with your fingers to ensure flat, uniform attachment.
4. Lightly brush the attached lithium disc face one final time. Place this whole spacer/lithium assembly onto the hotplate to preheat.
5. Using tweezers, grab the outer edge only of the preheated spacer/lithium assembly and place it centered inside the cell top part on top of the spring (lithium facing up).
6. Using a spatula, drop the liquefied molten salt electrolyte directly onto the exposed lithium metal surface.
7. Place a circular piece of separator onto the hotplate. As it heats, it will warp slightly into an arch-shape.
8. Transfer the separator onto the molten salt layer, ensuring the arch-curved side faces downward. Gently drag the separator from the outer edge of the cell toward the center to lay it flat and avoid bubbles.
9. Deposit a second drop of molten salt onto the top surface of the separator using a spatula.
10. Center your prepared cathode on top of the salted separator, ensuring the shiny Aluminum (Al) current collector side faces up.
11. Position an 18 mm round Aluminum disc current collector extension directly over the cathode, ensuring its shiny side faces downward.
12. Take the Al-coated bottom part (+) and write the sample name on the exterior casing using a marker.
13. Secure the labeled bottom part with metal tweezers, place it down square onto the cell stack, and press downward firmly.
14. Using non-conductive plastic tweezers, pick up the entire cell assembly and place it square into the crimper die (bottom part facing down).
15. Press the **START** button and verify that the green operation light illuminates. Once released, store the finished cell in the designated dried tray compartment.

   For a more detailed guide, please consult our full `Li Metal Full Coin Cells with Molten Salt SOP on Box <https://uchicago.box.com/s/bzidc59cs4z1nlahdssm5e5vkxzrpd3y>`_.

2. Li|Al Coin Cells with Molten Salt
-----------------------------------

**Thermal Setup:** Set the hotplate temperature according to the melting point of your specific salt system. Place the molten salt vial on the hotplate to liquefy the electrolyte.

**Assembly Steps:**

1. Lay down the cell top part (-) flat on the assembly surface.
2. Place one spring directly into the center of the top part.
3. Place a base spacer flat on top of the spring, ensuring the curved edge side faces down. Verify it is perfectly centered.
4. Take a second spacer, press your punched 12 mm lithium disc firmly onto its exact center using your hands, and brush the lithium surface one final time. Place this assembly onto the hotplate to preheat.
5. Grab the outer edge of the preheated lithium/spacer assembly and place it centered onto the base spacer inside the cell stack (lithium facing up).
6. Using a spatula, drop liquefied molten salt directly onto the hot lithium disc.
7. Place a piece of separator onto the hotplate until it deforms into an arch-shape.
8. Lay the separator down over the molten salt layer, dragging it cleanly from the outer edge of the cell to the center to prevent bubbles.
9. Apply a second drop of molten salt onto the top face of the separator layer using a spatula.
10. Place an 18 mm round Aluminum disc current collector extension onto the salted separator layer, ensuring the shiny side faces down.
11. Take the cell bottom part (+) and write the sample name on the exterior casing using a marker.
12. Secure the bottom part with metal tweezers, place it down square onto the cell stack, and press downward firmly.
13. Using non-conductive plastic tweezers, lift the cell assembly and seat it in the crimper die (bottom part facing down).
14. Press the **START** button (verify green light activation). Store the finalized coin cell in the designated dried tray compartment.

.. note::
   For a more detailed guide, please consult our full `Li|Al Cells with Molten Salt SOP on Box <https://uchicago.box.com/s/jvf8z0j32jitimv8cp27eepnn8v9bzhq>`_.

3. Li|Li Coin Cells with Molten Salt
-----------------------------------

**Thermal Setup:** Set the hotplate temperature according to the melting point of your specific salt system. Place the molten salt vial on the hotplate to liquefy the electrolyte.

**Assembly Steps:**

1. Lay down the cell top part (-) flat on the assembly surface.
2. Place one spring directly into the center of the top part.
3. Take a primary spacer, press your punched 12 mm lithium disc firmly onto its exact center using your hands, and brush the lithium surface one final time. Place this assembly onto the hotplate to preheat.
4. Grab the outer edge of the preheated lithium/spacer assembly and place it centered onto the spring inside the cell stack (lithium facing up).
5. Using a spatula, drop liquefied molten salt directly onto the hot lithium disc.
6. Place a piece of separator onto the hotplate until it deforms into an arch-shape.
7. Lay the separator down over the molten salt layer, ensuring the arch-curved side faces downward. Drag it cleanly from the outer edge of the cell to the center to prevent bubbles.
8. Apply a second drop of molten salt onto the top face of the separator layer using a spatula.
9. Prepare a secondary matching 12 mm lithium metal disc. Using tweezers, grab the disc by the edge and place it down perfectly centered on top of the salted separator layer.
10. Place a secondary matching spacer onto the hotplate to preheat. Once hot, pick it up with tweezers and place it flat directly on top of the upper lithium disc.
11. Secure the cell bottom part (+) with metal tweezers, fit it square over the entire internal stack, and press down firmly.
12. Using non-conductive plastic tweezers, transfer the full cell assembly to the crimper die (bottom part facing down).
13. Press the **START** button and verify that the green operation light illuminates. Place the finished symmetrical cell inside the designated dried tray compartment.
